The
program

A public workshop season by School of Tomorrow's AI — not the School itself, but one of the ways its research meets other people.

What it is

Room for Interaction is a series of workshops where the space itself becomes a playground for the body. Each session invites you to experiment with simple, creative interactions — voices that make paper flowers bloom, gestures that become sculptures, touches that light up space.

You don't need to code. You need curiosity.

The season

Seven independent, one-day workshops across 2026. Each workshop includes two short online prep sessions that introduce the ideas and tools, followed by an in-person Saturday in the atelier. Join online, in person, or both — choose how deep you want to dive.

Typical format: online Tuesdays, 19:00–21:00 CET; in person Saturday, 10:00–17:00 CET. Details can vary by session.

"You'll leave each session with hands-on prototypes, new tools to play with, and a shared sense of how technology becomes a tool for imagination, play, and radical experimentation."
— Room for Interaction, 2026

Where it

The Atelier
School of Tomorrow's AI Schönaustrasse 13 8620 Wetzikon, Zürich Schönau Areal
Open in Google Maps ↗︎

In-person sessions take place at the School of Tomorrow's AI Atelier at Schönau Areal in Wetzikon, in the Zürich region of Switzerland. The atelier is the current physical base: a place for building, testing and public experiments.

Online sessions happen ahead of each Saturday, introducing the ideas and tools before the room is used.

Room for Interaction was presented with Glitch Lab as a historical collaborator when the program was conceived. Glitch Lab is not the current venue.

Founded by Lina Lopes in September 2025 in Wetzikon, Switzerland, School of Tomorrow’s AI brings together artistic research, science and creative technology in a physical environment for experimentation. It builds on her earlier atelier practices in Brazil and emerged in a new technological context shaped by the expansion of artificial intelligence.

## If machines are learning, what are we teaching them?

The name plays on the idea of machine learning: if machines learn, what are we teaching them? Artificial intelligence is an invitation to explore human intelligence, life, materiality and technology. It can be a tool, a medium, a material or a subject of inquiry. Embodied AI is one of the School’s areas of interest.

## Research that starts in the world

Research often starts with encounters, conversations and lived experiences. Questions become experiments, mechanisms, objects and installations through speculative thinking and hands-on work. Material experimentation and speculative prototyping keep those questions physical.

## Open research

Open research, open-source practices and documentation create opportunities for others to learn from and contribute to these processes. Independent notebooks, repositories and publications remain independent; this site links to them rather than replacing them.

## Founder & Research Lead

Lina Lopes — artist, creative technologist, researcher and educator — founded the School and leads its research. The atelier is based in Wetzikon-Zürich.

## The School
Founded by Lina Lopes in September 2025 in Wetzikon, Switzerland, School of Tomorrow’s AI brings together artistic research, science and creative technology in a physical environment for experimentation. It builds on her earlier atelier practices in Brazil and emerged in a new technological context shaped by the expansion of artificial intelligence.

The name plays on the idea of machine learning: if machines learn, what are we teaching them? Artificial intelligence is an invitation to explore human intelligence, life, materiality and technology. It can be a tool, a medium or a subject of inquiry. Embodied AI is one of the School’s areas of interest.

Research often starts with encounters, conversations and lived experiences. Questions become experiments, mechanisms, objects and installations through speculative thinking and hands-on work. Open research, open-source practices and documentation create opportunities for others to learn from and contribute to these processes.

## The Atelier
The atelier is organised around different ways of making and thinking. Its workstations support interconnected investigations rather than operating as isolated departments.

| Station | Public description |
|---|---|
| BioLab | Bioart, living systems and biological experimentation. |
| Makerspace | Digital fabrication, 3D printing, laser cutting and rapid prototyping. |
| Dirty Workshop | Woodworking, cutting, drilling, construction and machine hacking. |
| Electronics Bench | Electronics, PCBs, circuit prototyping and embedded systems. |
| Wearables Lab | Wearable electronics, e-textiles and body–technology interfaces. |
| Craft Office | Paper, crafts, printing and material experimentation. |
| Studio | Photography, lighting, process documentation and instructional media. |
| Saloon | Hands-on experimentation, assembly, installations and prototypes in progress. |

Electronics Bench and Wearables Lab share a physical area but remain separate editorial entries. Inverted Gallery is not a research workstation.

## Inverted Gallery
**A gallery inside a working atelier.**

Inverted Gallery reverses the usual relationship between studio and exhibition space. Visitors pass through part of an active research atelier before reaching the gallery, encountering the environment in which ideas are made before encountering the space in which work is presented.

Managed by School of Tomorrow’s AI, the gallery welcomes inquiries for exhibitions, rehearsals, events and other cultural activities.

## Collaborate
Research grows through exchange. We welcome artists, researchers, students, laboratories, cultural institutions, companies and individuals interested in expanding what can be investigated, made and learned together.

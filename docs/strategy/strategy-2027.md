# Strategy 2027

## Identity
School of Tomorrow’s AI is an independent physical atelier for experimental research in art, science and creative technology, founded by Lina Lopes in September 2025 in Wetzikon, Switzerland. Public location wording: Wetzikon-Zürich, Switzerland. The atelier continues methodological practices developed in Brazil, in a new context following the rise of generative AI around 2023. It is currently operated by Lina as an individual; do not imply nonprofit, association, permanent staff, or tax-deductibility status.

Existing tagline: **Strange questions, made material.**

The name School plays on machine learning and the question of what we teach tomorrow’s AI. AI is an entry point for questions about intelligence, humanity, life, matter and technology; it can be a tool, medium, material or research subject. Embodied AI is an explicit interest, not a requirement that every project use AI or robotics. Avoid fear-based narratives and simplistic contrasts between screens and bodies. Human tool-making and curiosity are positive starting points.

Research often begins in conversations, encounters and lived experiences, then develops through speculative questions, material experiments, fabrication, documentation and exchange. This is a description of practice, not a rigid method. The physical atelier embodies this way of working.

## 2027 priorities
- Artificial Emotion is the flagship research project, with Soft Mechanisms, Kinetic Studies and Biosignals as research threads. Expected outputs may include exhibitions, installations, articles, publications, prototypes and open documentation; these are intentions, not completed deliverables.
- Distinguish **research** (ongoing investigation), **atelier** (physical research infrastructure and making practices), **programme** (thematic public framework and encounters), **publications** (authored knowledge and artefacts made available beyond their original research or activity context), **activity formats** (recurring ways of sharing research), and **events** (individual scheduled or completed activities). Artificial Emotion’s January–June 2027 period on Programme describes the public programme semester, not necessarily the full duration of the research itself.
- **Publishing** is a form of opening up research: interactive experiences, research boards, tools, essays, instructions and material artefacts may all count as publications when they are selected, authored outputs. A project may appear in both Research and Publications when the editorial purpose differs. Publications are not a course catalogue, shop or academic journal.
- Open research and open source are explicit commitments; use appropriate licenses and permissions, particularly for personal data and third-party materials. Public accessibility does not automatically mean openly licensed.
- Welcome independent research proposals, student projects, visits by arrangement and collaborative investigations. Workshops emerge from research; Open Research Studio is a recurring format when feasible, not a promised calendar.
- Announce Inverted Gallery, a cultural and multi-use space inside the atelier. Only the Inverted Gallery is available for rental. The research atelier and its workstations are not rental spaces. Seek an associated curator interested in art, science and technology. It may develop independent programming; the School manages the space. Maintain the existing brand for now.
- Build a network of exchange: access to equipment and research facilities, loans, materials, knowledge, hands-on assistance, financial contributions, commissioned research and institutional partnerships.
- Curate a modest archive of prior work, linking outward to original documentation. Do not treat every personal artwork or infrastructure project as institutional research.

## Governance and boundaries
Lina is founder and research lead; link to her independent website for credentials and portfolio. Do not announce unconfirmed organisational or academic plans. No promise of unrestricted access, formal supervision, residencies or regular classes. Credit contributors with explicit permission; anonymous contributions welcome. Publish campaign targets transparently when confirmed, without disclosing personal donor details. Preserve independent repositories and use hyperlinks rather than content replication.

## Success measures (proposed, no numerical targets yet)
Relevant collaboration inquiries, research funding conversations, useful in-kind contributions, student proposals, gallery inquiries, visits, and a manageable editorial workflow. Review qualitative outcomes rather than optimizing solely for traffic.

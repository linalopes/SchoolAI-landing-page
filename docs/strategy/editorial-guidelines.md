# Editorial guidelines

## Voice
English only, including Inverted Gallery. Clear, curious, concrete, experimental, understated. Use evidence of making and research rather than generic innovation language. Avoid fear-driven AI rhetoric, inflated claims, invented affiliations and vague institutional plural implying staff. Use “the School” for the institution and “we” only where appropriate to the actual collaboration. Keep the existing tagline and brand typography, colors, patterns and hero composition.

## Vocabulary
School of Tomorrow’s AI; Wetzikon-Zürich; Artificial Emotion (singular); Embodied AI; Open Research Studio; Research Wishlist; Inverted Gallery; Publications; BioLab; Makerspace; Dirty Workshop; Electronics Bench; Wearables Lab; Craft Office; Studio; Saloon. Electronics Bench and Wearables Lab are separate editorial workstations that share a physical area. Inverted Gallery is not a research workstation. Prefer Bio-signals for Artificial Emotion research threads unless the research notebook establishes a different official spelling. Preserve original project titles. Do not invent project details based on repository names. Do not invent publication dates, ISBNs, DOIs, edition numbers, licensing terms or authorship details.

## Content principles
- Short institutional introduction first; deeper context on relevant pages.
- Real photographs only, externally hosted. Every image needs meaningful alt text, permission and credit where applicable; decorative images have empty alt text.
- Links to personal blog, research notebook, repositories and event registrations are intentional. No duplication of full external articles.
- Differentiate institutional research, personal artistic practice, research tools, infrastructure development and **publications** (authored outputs made available beyond their original context). Examples belong only where they establish capability or research history.
- Publications may be interactive experiences, research boards, tools, essays, instructions or material artefacts. Present them as a curated editorial collection — not a course catalogue, resource directory, shop or journal.
- Open research and open source: link to actual documentation and licenses; avoid claiming every asset is openly licensed. Public accessibility does not mean openly licensed.
- Dates and statuses must be accurate. Future outcomes are aims, not achievements. Avoid unconfirmed events, supporters, curators and funding claims.
- Gallery copy: explain the inversion (visitors traverse the atelier before reaching exhibition space); offer inquiries for exhibitions, rehearsals and events; do not promise availability or quote unapproved prices.
- Contributors: text names only, optional organization links with permission. No portraits, personal contact details, private permissions records or individual donation amounts in public data.

## Editorial maintenance
Markdown for institutional pages, homepage copy, gallery rates, financial support, footer, location, wishlist **page labels** and **publication records**. Google Sheets for hero, events, archive, workstations, contributors and wishlist **records**. Publications do not use a Sheets feed at this stage. Each fact has one canonical source — never duplicate the same sentence in Markdown and Astro. Use draft/published status and review before publishing. Newsletter occasional, not scheduled by default. Owners edit Markdown or Sheets; they should not need to open component source for copy changes. See [content management](../editorial/content-management.md).

# Editorial guidelines

## Voice
English only, including Inverted Gallery. Clear, curious, concrete, experimental, understated. Use evidence of making and research rather than generic innovation language. Avoid fear-driven AI rhetoric, inflated claims, invented affiliations and vague institutional plural implying staff. Use “the School” for the institution and “we” only where appropriate to the actual collaboration. Keep the existing tagline and brand typography, colors, patterns and hero composition.

## Vocabulary
School of Tomorrow’s AI; Wetzikon-Zürich; Artificial Emotion (singular); Embodied AI; Open Research Studio; Research Wishlist; Inverted Gallery; BioLab; Makerspace (working label); Dirty Workshop; Electronics Bench & Wearables Lab (one area); Office; Studio; Saloon. Use Biosignals consistently unless the research notebook establishes a different official spelling. Preserve original project titles. Do not invent project details based on repository names.

## Content principles
- Short institutional introduction first; deeper context on relevant pages.
- Real photographs only, externally hosted. Every image needs meaningful alt text, permission and credit where applicable; decorative images have empty alt text.
- Links to personal blog, research notebook, repositories and event registrations are intentional. No duplication of full external articles.
- Differentiate institutional research, personal artistic practice, research tools and infrastructure development. Examples belong only where they establish capability or research history.
- Open research and open source: link to actual documentation and licenses; avoid claiming every asset is openly licensed.
- Dates and statuses must be accurate. Future outcomes are aims, not achievements. Avoid unconfirmed events, supporters, curators and funding claims.
- Gallery copy: explain the inversion (visitors traverse the atelier before reaching exhibition space); offer inquiries for exhibitions, rehearsals and events; do not promise availability or quote unapproved prices.
- Contributors: text names only, optional organization links with permission. No portraits, personal contact details, private permissions records or individual donation amounts in public data.

## Editorial maintenance
Markdown for stable narrative and wishlist; Google Sheets for frequently edited short texts, image URLs, updates, events, curated archive metadata and public credits. Each fact has one canonical source. Use draft/published status and review before publishing. Newsletter occasional, not scheduled by default.
